## Needs

- Air
- Water, protein, carbohydrates, lipids, minerals, vitamins, fiber
- Routine completion of physiological processes
- Comfort
- Protection
- Knowledge
- Skill

## Common Values

- Acceptance
- Acknowledgement
- Affection
- Autonomy
- Collaboration
- Encouragement
- Consistency / Order
- Novelty / Variety
- Space
- Support
- Trust

For a higher level of abstraction, see [Fundamental Human Motivations]({% link motivations.md %}).
