description: Enlightenment through intellectual honesty

The Zen Teachings of Bodhidharma proposes that "Not creating delusions is enlightenment." That got me thinking about what questions keep our own thinking honest and free of delusions.

- What do I really know?
- What am I assuming?
- What am I projecting?
- What do these abstract concepts actually point to?
- Am I collapsing spectrums into dichotomies?
- Am I imposing my subjective views as "objective facts"? 
- Am I transforming dynamic processes into static things?
